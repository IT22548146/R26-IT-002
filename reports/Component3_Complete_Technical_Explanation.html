1. Component 3 overview

Component 3 — Emergency Detection and Production Risk Management
Confirmed bulk order එකක් දෛනිකව monitor කර production emergency සහ schedule risk කලින් හඳුනාගෙන, managerට ක්‍රියාත්මක කළ හැකි recovery recommendation ලබාදීම මෙහි අරමුණයි.

Component 3 මඟින් සිදු කරන ප්‍රධාන කාර්යයන්:

  • ඉදිරි production days තුනේ machine, quality සහ schedule emergencies predict කිරීම.

8. Early-warning mechanism

Early-warning inference එක current day එක සමඟ previous daily monitoring history එකත් භාවිත කරනවා. එමඟින් ඉදිරි production days තුන තුළ subtype emergency ඇතිවීමේ probability එක ලබාදෙනවා.

Logistic Regression model එක feature weights එකතු කර sigmoid function එකෙන් probability එකක් ලබාදෙනවා:

z = intercept + feature₁×weight₁ + feature₂×weight₂ + ...
Probability = 1 / (1 + e⁻ᶻ)

Universal 50% threshold එකක් assume කරන්නේ නැහැ. Order-grouped validation මත Macro-F1 උපරිම වන target-specific calibrated thresholds භාවිත කරනවා.

10. Recovery planning

Recovery plan වෙනම ML model එකක් නොව explainable deterministic capacity engine එකකි. එය remaining quantity, available days, required rate, workers, machines සහ overtime limit සලකා බලනවා.

Possible recommendations:

  • Continue the current production plan.
  • Increase working hours within the declared overtime limit.
  • Reassign available workers.
  • Activate feasible backup machines.
  • Improve daily production efficiency.
  • Escalate or reallocate when the deadline cannot be recovered locally.

මෙය rule-based කිරීමෙන් recommendation එක explainable, capacity-safe සහ physically executable වෙනවා.

20. Likely panel questions and concise answers

Q: What is Component 3?
A: Component 3 is the daily emergency-detection and production-risk-management component. It predicts current and future risks and generates an explainable recovery plan.
Q: How does C2 connect to C3?
A: C2 planning results and plant allocations are stored in the shared database. The authenticated plant manager selects an assigned order, and its dates, quantity, commitment and production-day details automatically populate C3.
Q: How many active ML artifacts are used?
A: Five: two main risk models and three next-three-day early-warning models.
Q: Why use Random Forest?
A: It captured nonlinear interactions among output, damage, resources and schedule features and achieved the strongest grouped Macro-F1 for current and overall risk.
Q: Why use Logistic Regression?
A: The regularised linear model generalised better for selected small and imbalanced early-warning targets and provides stable probabilities.
Q: Why is Macro-F1 important?
A: Accuracy can appear high when one class dominates. Macro-F1 gives emergency and non-emergency classes equal importance.
Q: How was leakage prevented?
A: Complete bulk orders were kept together using Bulk Order ID as the grouping boundary. An outer test order could not influence training, tuning or threshold selection.
Q: Is the recovery plan generated by ML?
A: No. ML detects risk; the recovery engine uses deterministic, capacity-aware calculations so the recommendation remains explainable and executable.
Q: How does C3 connect to C4?
A: Saved C3 daily records are aggregated by order and plant after completion. Risk counts and operational KPIs are then sent to C4 for performance scoring.
Q: Does saving a daily record retrain the model?
A: No. Runtime saves operational evidence. Retraining is a separate controlled offline process using verified outcomes.
Q: Why are there two databases?
A: The main database supports the integrated operational workflow, while the C3 tracking database preserves research-grade monitoring, verification and recovery audit history.

One-minute presentation answer

“My Component 3 is a hybrid production-risk monitoring and emergency-management module. Component 2 assigns a confirmed bulk order to a plant and provides its production plan, dates and daily commitment. The plant manager records actual daily output, damage, breakdowns and worker shortages. Five active ML artifacts predict the current risk type, overall deadline risk and three categories of early warnings. These predictions are combined with deterministic deadline and capacity rules to determine severity and generate an explainable recovery plan. Official daily records, verified outcomes and recovery actions are stored by order and plant. After completion, aggregated Component 3 risk data is passed to Component 4 for plant-performance analysis.”
